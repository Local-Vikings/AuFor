Solar forecast

Uses Open-Meteo weather data and pvlib physics to predict hourly AC power output for 1 to 30 days ahead. Configure every panel group separately.

  • Hourly AC output per panel group
  • Daily kWh totals
  • Cloud & temperature effects
⌁

Battery simulation

Simulates your battery's state of charge hour by hour against your household load. See exactly when you charge, discharge, and grid-import.

  • Hourly SoC tracking
  • Configurable DoD & efficiency
  • Grid export detection
◎

Decision layer

Rule-based recommendations telling you what to do each hour: run the dishwasher, pre-charge the battery, or hold off and buy cheap grid energy.

  • Use / Direct / Store / Optimize
  • Timestamped actions
  • Honest about simulated data
⟶

How it works

Pick your site on the map → configure your panels, battery, and daily load → hit Run energy forecast. The app fetches real weather, runs the physics, and gives you a 24 h–30 day plan.

Location→ Weather→ Physics (pvlib)→ Battery sim→ Decision
